My Profile

The Professional Behind the Work

Combining construction knowledge, commercial awareness and practical project experience.

Building a career around construction, cost and delivery.

I am a Quantity Surveyor and Construction Management professional with a Bachelor of Construction.

My professional development has provided exposure to quantity surveying, construction management, cost control, tender documentation, site activities and project administration.

My experience includes preparation and review of Bills of Quantities, quantity take-offs, valuations, payment certificates, variation orders, tender evaluation, cost control, contract administration and project close-out.
